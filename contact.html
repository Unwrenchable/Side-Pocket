Quick Answers

Frequently Asked

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping is 3–7 business days. Expedited 2-day options are available at checkout. All orders $75+ ship free.

What's your return policy?

30-day returns on unworn items with tags attached. Start a return from your order confirmation email.

How do I find my size?

Every product page has a size guide. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for hoodies and tees.

Do you do wholesale?

Yes! Email us at hello@sidepocketapparel.com with your store info and minimum order quantities.

Can I track my order?

Absolutely. You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships, usually within 1–2 business days.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship within the United States. International shipping is coming soon — follow us for updates.
