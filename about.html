Our Mission

More Than Clothing

We started Side Pocket because we were tired of choosing between quality and style. Too many brands cut corners on fabric or go overboard on branding. We decided to do both right.

Our designs are made to last — not just one season. We source premium materials, work with skilled craftspeople, and only release pieces we'd actually wear ourselves.

Side Pocket is for the people who move differently. Who don't need to shout to be heard. Who carry their confidence in every pocket.

The Journey

How We Got Here

2018

The Beginning

Side Pocket launched with a small capsule collection — 3 tee designs and a hoodie. Sold out in 72 hours from a single Instagram post.

2019

Building the Community

We expanded into headwear and bottoms, and started showing up at local pop-ups. Our customer base grew from a few hundred to over 5,000 loyal fans.

2021

Going Online

Launched the first official e-commerce store. Fulfilled orders in all 50 states within the first month. The Side Pocket community was officially nationwide.

2023

10,000 Customers Strong

Hit a major milestone — 10,000 customers served. Dropped our biggest collection yet and introduced the signature SP logo that now lives on every piece.

2025

What's Next

New silhouettes, new collabs, and a bigger platform. The mission stays the same: premium gear built for people who move differently.
